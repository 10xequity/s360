How it works

Show up. We run it.

  • Arrive. Your coach meets the group at the front desk inside FieldhouseUSA and gets everyone on the courts.
  • Play. Warm-up games, then the Splash Meter shooting contest: every shot's arc, depth and alignment scored live, names on the leaderboard.
  • Compete. Skill-court challenges, dribbling and passing games against the clock and each other.
  • Celebrate. Table time for food and cake. You bring the food; we handle the setup.

Ages, skill levels and mixed groups are all fine; the coach scales the games to the room. Every guest's parent or guardian signs the participation waiver before playing.
