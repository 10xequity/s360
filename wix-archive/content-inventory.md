Shoot 360’s proprietary shooting technology, utilized by nearly every NBA team and over 100 collegiate programs, transforms the court into a high-performance laboratory. At the core of this system is the Splash Meter™, a patented motion-tracking interface that provides instant, objective feedback on the three critical components of a perfect shot: arc, depth, and left/right alignment. By capturing these metrics in real-time, the system identifies the "Splash Zone"—the optimal target area that yields a 98% make probability. This data-driven approach removes the guesswork from training, allowing athletes to make micro-adjustments on the fly and build elite muscle memory through high-repetition, clinical calibration.

#### BALL SKILLS SIMULATOR
30
Dribbling Skills Games
Passing Skills Games
The skills technology at Shoot 360 features the world’s first high-tech passing and ball-handling cages, designed to digitize and quantify an athlete’s technical proficiency. Utilizing advanced computer vision and sensor technology, the Skill Cages guide players through gamified, interactive workouts led by virtual trainers. These sessions measure critical performance markers such as ball speed, reaction time, body positioning, and passing accuracy with both hands. By merging physical activity with digital gamification, the technology creates a "video-game-like" environment where athletes can virtually compete against others globally while receiving a personalized roadmap of their technical development.

Shoot 360’s advanced sensor technology utilizes a network of machine-vision cameras and patented ball-tracking sensors to digitize every movement on the court. By capturing high-fidelity data on ball velocity, spin rates, and point-of-force impact, the system provides objective, clinical metrics that traditional coaching cannot see. This "air-traffic control" for basketball ensures that every rep is measured with professional-grade precision, turning raw effort into a predictable, data-backed science.

### SHOOT 360
THE WORLD’S LEADING IMMERSIVE BASKETBALL TRAINING EXPERIENCE
The world’s leading immersive basketball training experience that combines in-person skill development with digital gamification and virtual competition.
​
At Shoot 360 you will train with our coaches, measure your skills with our proprietary technology, and track your progress on the Shoot 360 mobile app.
SHOOTING
Unlock your shooting potential using our dedicated shooting courts and our patented Splash Meter™. You’ll accelerate your shooting development through high repetition and getting real-time feedback and using our industry leading technology.
BALL HANDLING
Improve your ball-handling skills with our virtual trainer who will lead you through a series of guided workouts designed to improve body positioning, ball speed, ball accuracy, and ball control with either hand.
SPLASH ZONE
™ TECHNOLOGY
The Splash Zone™, a key feature of Shoot 360’s technology, represents the optimal target area for a basketball shot, achieved when the shot's arc, depth, and alignment align perfectly.
Players are guided to consistently land their shots within this zone using real-time feedback from the Splash Meter™. When all three metrics are within the Splash Zone™, the probability of making the shot increases dramatically, reaching as high as 98%.
This data-driven tool is designed to help athletes refine their shooting mechanics and achieve precision through measurable improvements, whether they are training for professional, collegiate, or high school competition.
CAMPS, CLASSES, CLINICS & YOUTH TRAINING
We offer group training opportunities for all ages and skill levels to build well-rounded fundamental skills. Our camps, classes, and clinics cover a wide range of skill development topics, taught by certified Shoot 360 coaches.
