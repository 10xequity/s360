## HQ brand facts (shoot360.com, Squarespace)

- **Taglines**: "GET BETTER FASTER." · "TRY IT FOR FREE TODAY." · "THE MOST ADVANCED BASKETBALL TRAINING FACILITY IN THE WORLD" · "START TRACKING YOUR PERFORMANCE TODAY." Franchise site: "The World Leader in technology-based Basketball Training"; values "E.P.I.C — Ethics, Performance, Innovation, Culture of Encouragement".
- **Fonts/colours** (HQ CSS): body Open Sans, display Rajdhani; primary red `#ef001d` (variants `#d8001a`, `#cc0017`), black, white, greys. Every franchise sampled uses red/black/white.
- **Technology names**: Splash Meter™ (arc, depth, alignment), Splash Zone™ (up to 98% make probability), dedicated shooting courts with automatic rebounding ("300 shots in 30 minutes"), passing skill court, ball-handling virtual-trainer court, Shoot 360 Mobile App (stats, leaderboards, booking 7 days ahead, league registration), Shoot 360 Leagues, "over 25 patents" (franchise site). The phrase "Ball Skills Simulator" on the Denver Wix site appears nowhere else.
- **Standard tier names**: Rookie (4) · Pro (8) · Pro+ · All-Star (unlimited) · All-Star+ · Hall of Fame (unlimited, 12-mo, "most popular") · 12 Visits · Guest Workout.
- **Free-workout terms** (HQ trial page): first-time visitors and local residents; valid at participating facilities; conditions apply. Location pages add: photo ID, guardian present for minors, typically within 25 miles.
- **Offerings taxonomy**: Shooting · Passing · Ball Handling · Group Training (camps, classes, clinics) · Personal Training · Events (birthday and corporate) · Leagues.
- **Shared back end**: every location's trial/membership CTA routes to shoot360-prod.saloncloudsplus.io and/or shoot360.com/trial | membership-pricing | membership-checkout with a 7-digit `locationId`. Denver's is 5739019.
- **Corporate**: founder Craig Moody, CEO Terry Michaelson; first location Beaverton OR, 2012; 17 HQ staff bios at shoot360.com/meet-the-team.

Unverified: Oakville content (script-rendered), several coach/testimonial subpages (401 or unknown slugs), Elfsight widget contents, image-only price menus at Vero Beach/Spokane/Pittsburgh, "28 of 30 NBA teams" and "NASA/Carnegie Mellon" claims on franchise sites.
