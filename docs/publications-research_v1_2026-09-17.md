## Two claims the site should not repeat as-is

1. **"29 of 30 NBA teams use Shoot 360's technology."** The NBA install count belongs to Noah Basketball's shot-tracking system, which Shoot 360 licenses. Noah's blog (2020-11-17) announces the licensing partnership; Franchise Times (2025-09-29) writes "the engine running Shoot 360 was developed by partner company Noah Basketball"; the Birmingham Squadron page says Noah is "partnered with 28 of 30 NBA teams". Safe wording: *the shot-tracking behind the Splash Meter is the same kind installed in most NBA practice facilities*. Solid team-level facts: Warriors (academy since 2020), Jazz (Lehi, 2022), Nets/Liberty (Brooklyn, 2025), Clippers (Torrance camps; Intuit Dome fan booth per Moody).
2. **"Over 30 patents."** Google Patents and FreePatentsOnline return no patent assigned to Shoot 360 or naming Craig Moody. The shot-tracking patents in play are Pillar Vision / Noah Basketball (US7094164B2, US10610757B1). The verifiable Shoot 360 IP is the SPLASH METER trademark (Shoot 360 Nation LLC, filed 2022-04-26, snippet only). HQ's own copy says "patented Splash Meter" and the franchisee is licensed to use HQ copy, so the site keeps that phrase; the publications page does not add to it.
