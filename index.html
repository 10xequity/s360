How it works

Three steps to a better shot

Come evaluate

Book an evaluation online. A coach meets you on the court, you shoot on the Splash Meter, and you leave with a baseline on arc, depth and alignment.

Train with a coach on the tech

Dedicated shooting courts and skill courts turn every session into 300+ measured reps. A coach reads the numbers with you; that coaching is included.

Track and book in the app

Every workout lands in the Shoot 360 app: shot charts, streaks, achievements, and the schedule where you register for classes, camps and leagues.

The technology

Fully integrated. Uncompromisingly accurate.

The patented Splash Meter™ reads the three things that decide whether a shot goes in: arc, depth, and left-right alignment. Put all three in the Splash Zone™ and the make probability climbs to 98%.

What changes when every rep is measured

Objective

No more guessing

A missed shot has a reason: arc, depth or alignment. The Splash Meter names it instantly, and the player fixes that one thing on the next rep.

300+

Automatic rebounding puts 300 or more shots into a 30-minute session, each one scored. That is a week of gym time compressed into half an hour.
