How it works

Three steps to a better shot

Book a free evaluation

Pick a time online. A coach meets you on the court, you shoot on the Splash Meter, and you leave with a baseline on arc, depth and alignment.

Train on the technology

Dedicated shooting courts and skill courts turn every session into 300+ measured reps. The machines rebound; you shoot.

Track it in the app

Every workout lands in the Shoot 360 app: shot charts, streaks, achievements, and a booking calendar for your next session.

The technology

Fully integrated. Uncompromisingly accurate.

The patented Splash Meter™ reads the three things that decide whether a shot goes in: arc, depth, and left-right alignment. Put all three in the Splash Zone™ and the make probability climbs to 98%.
