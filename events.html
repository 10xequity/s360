Special events

Team-building with a scoreboard

Corporate outings, partnership and client meetings, bachelor and bachelorette parties, graduations, end-of-season team nights, club and school takeovers. Everyone shoots on the Splash Meter, everyone gets a number, and the leaderboard settles it. Then the group moves to the private room or the restaurant and bar.

  • Evenings work. Special events run after regular training hours, so your group has the space to itself.
  • Meeting and party rooms for the sit-down part: presentations, food, presents or a toast.
  • Food and drinks from the on-site restaurant and bar, from pizza and wings to a full catering menu. No outside food or drink.
  • A coach runs the floor. Shooting contests, skill-court challenges, team relays. No basketball experience needed.

Birthday parties

The party with a leaderboard

Two courts, a Shoot 360 coach running the games, and every kid's shooting numbers up on the board at the end. Then the private party room for food, cake and presents. Any age that can shoot at a hoop; the coach scales the games to the group.

  • Arrive. Your coach meets the group at the front desk inside FieldhouseUSA and gets everyone on the courts.
  • Play. Warm-up games, then the Splash Meter shooting contest: arc, depth and alignment scored live, names on the board.
  • Compete. Skill-court challenges: dribbling and passing games against the clock and each other.
  • Celebrate. The private party room for the second hour. Food and cake are ordered from the on-site restaurant; we set up and clean up.

Party rules, in plain words

  • Food and drinks are ordered from the on-site restaurant and bar; no outside food, cake or drinks. Bottled drinks only in the party room; water only on the courts.
  • Court shoes for every athlete. No black-soled shoes or cleats on the courts.
  • Decorations are welcome in the party room as long as nothing sticks to the walls.
  • Every athlete's parent or guardian signs the participation waiver before playing; we send the link with your confirmation. Read the waiver →
  • Weekends fill first, so send your request early; we confirm within a business day and sort out the booking with you by phone or email.

Date nights

Dinner and a shootout

An hour for two on the Splash Meter and the skills court, a coach-run shootout with both your names on the leaderboard, then food and drinks at the restaurant and bar downstairs from the courts. Booked ahead, so the courts are ready when you arrive.

  • 60 minutes of court time: 30 on a shooting bay, 30 on a skills court, with a coach setting the games
  • Every shot scored on arc, depth and alignment; the loser buys the first round
  • Food and drinks from the on-site restaurant and bar, ordered on the night or reserved with your booking
  • Evenings, Wednesday to Saturday, by request; groups of two couples welcome
