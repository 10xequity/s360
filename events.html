How many people fit

Group size depends on the ages, because everyone needs their own station. Two courts hold ten athletes under 10, or six aged 11 to 17; all four courts double that. Adult groups are sized when you book, so tell us your headcount and we will tell you how many courts you need.

Birthday parties

The party with a leaderboard

Two courts, a Shoot 360 coach running the games, and every kid's shooting numbers up on the board at the end. Then the private party room for food, cake and presents. Any age that can shoot at a hoop; the coach scales the games to the group.

  • Arrive. Your coach meets the group at the front desk inside FieldhouseUSA and gets everyone on the courts.
  • Play. Warm-up games, then the Splash Meter shooting contest: arc, depth and alignment scored live, names on the board.
  • Compete. Skill-court challenges: dribbling and passing games against the clock and each other.
  • Celebrate. The private party room for the second hour. Food and cake are ordered from the on-site restaurant; we set up and clean up.

Party rules, in plain words

  • Food and drinks are ordered from the on-site restaurant and bar; no outside food, cake or drinks. Bottled drinks only in the party room; water only on the courts.
  • Court shoes for every athlete. No black-soled shoes or cleats on the courts.
  • Decorations are welcome in the party room as long as nothing sticks to the walls.
  • Every athlete's parent or guardian signs the participation waiver before playing; we send the link with your confirmation. Read the waiver →
  • Weekends fill first, so send your request early; we confirm within a business day and sort out the booking with you by phone or email.
