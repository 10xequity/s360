Privacy policy

Last updated: April 17, 2024

Your privacy is critical to us and it is important that you understand our practices and procedures regarding our use before disclosing information. The following privacy policy explains these practices and procedures, to which you are agreeing by accessing our site and services.

a. Personal information

“Personal Information” is information that allows us to identify you. The types of Personal Information that we may collect from you through your use of our site and services may include, without limitation, your name, e-mail address, phone number, and date of birth. We collect Personal Information from you in a variety of ways, including when you complete a form, visit our facilities, provide feedback, or interact with our digital and social media content.

b. Non-personal information

We may also collect information that does not identify you personally (“Non-Personal Information”). This includes information that is linked to your computer or mobile device. When you interact with our site or social media content, we may collect information about your computer that your browser sends, such as your IP address, browser type and language, access times, pages visited, and referring website addresses; information collected through “cookies” and third-party sources (e.g. Google Analytics). With the uses set forth in this policy, we may use Non-Personal Information we collect to analyze trends, track the movement of visitors, learn about and determine how much time visitors spend using the website, how visitors navigate the site, and to gather broad demographic information for aggregate use.

c. Social media

When using certain third-party social networking services (e.g., Facebook, X), you may have the option to share your Social Network information and activity with external sites (including Shoot360.com). Information we may access about you, with your consent, from a Social Network may include, but is not limited to, your basic Social Network information, your location data, your list of contacts, friends or followers and certain information about your activities on the Social Network. If you permit a connection between a Social Network and a service, we (and that Social Network) may be able to identify you and to associate information received pursuant to the connection with information we already have about you. For more information, please review the privacy disclosures and terms of your Social Network account, which govern how that account information is collected and shared with us.

d. Disclosing information

In order to administer the Services, we may share your information with third parties including without limitation: hosting providers, data management companies, address list hosting companies, e-mail service providers, analytics companies, distribution companies, fulfillment companies, payment processors, operational partners, business partners, and marketing partners.

We may also use your information for purposes including without limitation the following:

  • To contact you regarding our services
  • To provide you with information you have requested, or products or services you have signed up for
  • To administer your participation in a contest, sweepstakes, promotion, survey, poll or vote
  • To send you promotional material or special offers
  • To customize or personalize ads, offers and content made available to you based on your usage of the Services and analyze the performance of those ads, offers and content, as well as your interaction with them

For more information or questions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us.

Accessibility

Shoot 360 Denver wants everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA: the site is built with semantic HTML, works with a keyboard alone, keeps text contrast at 4.5:1 or better, provides text alternatives for images, and respects your system's reduced-motion setting.

If anything on the site is difficult to use, or you need information in another format, email info@shoot360denver.com or call 720-262-5501 and we will help.
