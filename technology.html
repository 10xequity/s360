The world's leading immersive basketball training experience: in-person skill development combined with digital gamification and virtual competition. You train with our coaches, measure your skills with proprietary technology, and track your progress in the Shoot 360 app.

Arc

Splash Meter™

Arc. Depth. Alignment.

Shoot 360's patented motion-tracking interface gives instant, objective feedback on the three components of a perfect shot. The technology is used by NBA teams and more than 100 collegiate programs.

Capturing those metrics in real time lets athletes make micro-adjustments on the fly and build elite muscle memory through high-repetition, clinical calibration. It removes the guesswork from training.

Depth

Splash Zone™

The 98% target

The Splash Zone™ is the optimal target area for a shot: the point where arc, depth and alignment line up. Players are guided to land shots in the zone with real-time feedback, and when all three metrics are in the zone, the probability of a make climbs as high as 98%.

On the dedicated shooting courts, automatic rebounding keeps the ball coming back so a 30-minute session means 300+ measured shots, whether you are training for professional, collegiate or high-school competition.

Skill courts

Passing and ball-handling, digitised

The world's first high-tech passing and ball-handling cages quantify an athlete's technical proficiency. Computer vision and sensors guide players through gamified workouts led by virtual trainers.

  • Measures ball speed, reaction time, body positioning and passing accuracy with both hands
  • 30 dribbling games and 30 passing games
  • Compete virtually against players across the Shoot 360 network
  • A personalised roadmap of your technical development

Advanced sensor technology

Air-traffic control for basketball

A network of machine-vision cameras and patented ball-tracking sensors digitises every movement on the court, capturing ball velocity, spin rate and point-of-force impact.

The result is objective, clinical metrics that traditional coaching cannot see. Every rep is measured with professional-grade precision, turning raw effort into a predictable, data-backed science.

Why it works

What the technology does for a player

Objective

Feedback you cannot argue with

Arc, depth and alignment are measured, not eyeballed. A player stops guessing why a shot missed and starts fixing the one thing the meter shows.

300+

More reps, each one corrected

Automatic rebounding means 300 or more shots in 30 minutes. Skill is repetition with correction, and this is the most correction per rep a player can get.

Faster

Muscle memory built on the right motion

Real-time feedback lets an athlete adjust mid-session, so the pattern that gets grooved is the correct one, instead of a bad habit reinforced for a season.

Both hands

Ball skills measured, not assumed

The skill courts time reaction, score passing accuracy with each hand and grade body position. Weak-hand work becomes a number that moves.

Gamified

Kids want to come back

Leaderboards, achievements and virtual competition across the network turn drills into games. Consistency is the whole battle in youth development.

Coached

A coach reads the data with you

The technology shows what happened; the coach shows why and what to change. At Shoot 360 Denver that coaching is included in every membership.
