A technology-based basketball training facility. Dedicated shooting courts with the patented Splash Meter™ give instant feedback on arc, depth and alignment; high-tech skill courts measure passing and ball-handling; and every rep is tracked in the Shoot 360 app. Coaches use the data to guide your training.

Players of all ages and skill levels, from youth learning fundamentals to high-school, college and adult athletes. Classes, camps and Little Ballers (ages 6–10) are grouped by age and level. A parent or guardian attends with minors for the first visit.

Book an evaluation session online and a coach meets you on the court. Evaluations are for first-time visitors; a parent or guardian attends with minors.

About an hour, coach-led. You shoot on the Splash Meter, work a skill court and leave with a baseline on your shot's arc, depth and alignment, all tracked in the app. There is no obligation to join.

Use the Book an Evaluation button on any page, or call 720-262-5501. Members book sessions, classes and personal training in the Shoot 360 app.

Arc, depth and left-right alignment on every shot, in real time. When all three land in the Splash Zone™ the make probability rises to as high as 98%. More on the technology →

A 30-minute hoop session on a shooting court produces 300 or more measured shots. Automatic rebounding keeps the ball coming back.

A session is an hour on the technology: 30 minutes on a shooting bay, where automatic rebounding puts 300 or more measured shots up, and 30 minutes on a skills court for passing and ball-handling, with your coach reading the numbers with you. That hour is what a membership counts as one session, so JV's four sessions a month are four hours on the floor.
