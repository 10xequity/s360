Included in every membership

Coaching is not an add-on here

Every Shoot 360 Denver membership includes pro training and oversight: a coach works with you during your sessions, reading the Splash Meter with you, correcting mechanics, and setting the skill courts to your level. There is no additional fee for coaching during a session.

Membership policies

The fine print, in plain words

Fees, tax and deposit

A one-time $40 enrollment fee creates your athlete profile and app connection (none for Drop-In or My Spark). Aurora sales tax is added to dues. One month's dues is held as a deposit and applied to your fourth month, or refunded if you leave after the commitment.

Commitments

JV, Varsity, Committed and Lock-In Reserved run on a 3-month commitment; Unlimited Classes on 1 month; My Spark on 6; Ball is Life on 12 in exchange for the lower unlimited rate. After the commitment, memberships continue month to month until you cancel in the app before the 1st. The contract you sign at signup is the binding version.

Booking and no-shows

Sessions are booked one at a time inside your tier's scheduling window; no back-to-back bookings. Cancel in the app before your session and nothing happens. A no-show with no call is a strike: the first adds $50 to your membership, the second ends it without refund.

Pausing a membership

Injury, travel, tournament season: memberships can be suspended rather than cancelled, and your commitment clock pauses with them. Ask the front desk before your billing date for the current terms and notice period.

Coaching sessions

Coaching during your sessions is included and never expires. Private one-on-one training is arranged individually with a coach rather than sold as a package.

Waiver and conduct

Every athlete, or a parent or guardian for a minor, signs the participation waiver once; it stays on file. Court shoes only on the courts, water only on the floor, and the coach's call is final. Read the waiver →
