Included in every membership

Coaching is not an add-on here

Every Shoot 360 Denver membership includes private training and coaching. A coach works with you during your sessions, reading the Splash Meter with you, correcting mechanics, and setting the skill courts to your level. There is no additional fee for coaching during a session and nothing extra to buy to be coached.

  • Coach on the floor during your sessions, at no extra charge
  • Pro, All-Star and Hall of Fame tiers add scheduled one-on-one personal coaching sessions on top
  • Your numbers follow you: coaches see the same app data you do, so the plan adjusts week to week

Membership policies

The fine print, in plain words

Pausing a membership

Injury, travel, tournament season: you can suspend your membership for up to TBD months per year for $TBD a month. Ask TBD days before your billing date and your commitment clock pauses with it.

Commitments

Rookie, Pro and All-Star run on a 3-month commitment; Hall of Fame on 12 months in exchange for the lower unlimited rate. After the commitment, memberships continue month to month. Contract terms live on Shoot 360's membership page when you join.

Coaching sessions

Coaching during your visits is included and never expires. Scheduled one-on-one sessions bundled with Pro, All-Star and Hall of Fame are use-it-or-lose-it each month, so book them in the app early.
