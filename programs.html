How registration works

Everything is booked in the Shoot 360 app

Members and non-members alike register for classes, camps, leagues and drop-in sessions in the app. Download it, create an athlete profile, and pick from the live schedule. Your workout stats, achievements and bookings all live in the same place.

Need a hand the first time? Call 720-262-5501 and the front desk will walk you through it.

Classes

Coach-led group training

Weekly classes for all ages and skill levels, taught by certified Shoot 360 coaches and built to develop well-rounded fundamental skills on the technology: shooting mechanics on the Splash Meter, passing and ball-handling on the skill courts, and game concepts in between.

  • Grouped by age and level; the live schedule is in the app
  • Unlimited Classes ($149/mo) and Little Ballers for ages 6–10: every class on the schedule, all month
  • JV, Varsity and Committed members: add unlimited classes for $40/mo; Ball is Life includes them

Register in the app. Every class, for members and non-members, is booked in the Shoot 360 app.

Camps

School breaks, summer, and Nike

Shoot 360 camps & clinics

Multi-day camps over school breaks and summer, plus single-day clinics and combines on specific skills. Full days on the technology with a coach-to-athlete ratio that keeps every kid shooting. Members get discounts on Nike Basketball Camps.

Register in the app. Camp dates and spots are posted in the Shoot 360 app.

Nike Basketball Camps

Official Nike camps run at Shoot 360 Denver each summer through US Sports Camps. Dates, ages and registration are on the camp page, not in the app.

Leagues

Weekly competition, tracked

Shoot 360 Leagues put small teams head to head on the technology, with every shot and skill-court score recorded, and a leaderboard that reaches across the whole Shoot 360 network. Local competition, virtual rivals.

Register in the app. League seasons, teams and schedules are in the Shoot 360 app.

Basketball club

Club basketball with Team EVO

Looking for a team? Shoot 360 Denver's club basketball runs through Team EVO: competitive club teams and skills training for players who want a season, not just sessions. Tryouts, rosters, schedules and registration are on the Team EVO site.

Private training

Coaching is already included in every membership. For dedicated one-on-one blocks beyond that, email us the athlete's age, level and availability and a coach will reply with options.

Homeschool families

Weekdays 2–4 PM, the courts are yours

Homeschool sessions run Monday to Friday from 2 to 4 PM, while the building is quiet. Full access to the Splash Meter shooting courts and the skill courts with a coach on the floor, and every rep tracked in the app for your records.

  • Monday – Friday, 2:00 – 4:00 PM
  • Members use their regular sessions; non-members book a Drop-In
  • Siblings welcome; ask about family pricing

Book in the app so a coach is ready for your group, or call 720-262-5501.
